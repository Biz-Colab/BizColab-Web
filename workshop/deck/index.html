Score your business
on three questions.

Give each one a number from 1 to 5. Be honest. Nobody sees your numbers.

1 = not at all5 = yes, every time
Question 1 · How you show up

Can a buyer find you?

  • You show up in search for what you do in your city
  • Your Google profile is complete and has recent reviews
  • Your LinkedIn and social profiles are current
  • ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude name you when asked who is best
Question 2 · Brand and communication

Does it all say the same thing?

  • A stranger can tell what you do and who it is for in 15 seconds
  • Your logo, type, color and voice match everywhere
  • Your website, profiles and emails tell the same story
Question 3 · Marketing and sales

Is the engine built to grow?

  • Your offer, your price and who it is for are clear
  • You know where your leads come from
  • You know what happens after a lead lands
