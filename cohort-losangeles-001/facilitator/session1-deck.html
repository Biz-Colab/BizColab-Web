Why I built this

Peers, not gurus.

What I learned the hard way

  • Every real unlock in my own businesses came from other operators in the room — not books, not consultants
  • The advice that actually worked came from someone one step ahead, in the trenches with me, not someone selling a framework
  • Most "communities" are content feeds with a Slack bolted on — nobody's actually accountable to anybody

So I built the room

  • A room built for that on purpose — not networking, not content, not a course
  • Just people pushing each other's businesses forward, on a clock, every Monday
  • Small by design: six seats, one cohort, so every problem gets real attention

House rules

Six things that make this work.

Show up

All four Mondays. Missing one costs the room more than it costs you.

The room stays in the room

Numbers, deals, struggles — nothing leaves without permission.

Experience over advice

"Here's what happened when I tried it" beats "you should."

Make specific asks

"Help me grow" gets nothing. "Who's hired a fractional CFO?" gets three names.

Give first

Offer the intro, the template, the contact before you're asked for it.

Notes, not recordings

A note-taker writes up decisions, asks and commitments. No video, no audio.

Your three minutes

Three questions, that's it.

01 · ~60 sec

What you do

  • The business, who it's for, roughly how big
  • Skip the origin story — save it for Slack
02 · ~90 sec

Your one challenge

  • The thing actually keeping you stuck — pick one
  • Say where the number sits today
03 · ~30 sec

What would help most

  • The one kind of help you'd take right now
  • The more specific it is, the more this room can do with it

Hard timer — 3 minutes. Hold your offers and advice for the open forum after the break; that's what the back half of the night is for.

Your one challenge

From X to Y, by when.

Too vague

Won't survive four weeks

  • "Grow revenue"
  • "Get better at marketing"
  • "Figure out my pricing"
  • Nobody can help you, and you can't tell if it moved
Specific enough

The room can act on this

  • "From 2 inbound leads/month to 8, by Sep 14"
  • "Land one $15–20K client from a warm intro"
  • "Ship the new pricing page and test it on 10 calls"
  • Where it sits today, where you want it, and by when

Write down today's number when you say it — that's what we measure against on Sep 14. Not sure yet? Say your best version out loud and sharpen it in Slack by Wednesday. Naming it beats perfecting it.
