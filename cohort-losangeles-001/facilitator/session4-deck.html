What tonight is

Honest beats impressive.

Not the point

Looking good in the room

  • Reframing a flat number as "a lot of learning"
  • Reporting on activity instead of outcome
  • Quietly swapping in an easier goal than the one you named
  • Nobody here is your investor. There's nothing to sell tonight
The point

Knowing what actually happened

  • The number then, the number now — even if it's the same
  • What shipped, and what you didn't get to
  • The real reason it didn't — capacity, clarity, fear, or the wrong bet
  • A "didn't move, and here's exactly why" is the most useful thing you can give this room

Four weeks moves some things and not others. The value is in knowing which, and why — that's what makes the next four weeks different.

Feedback is held during the scoreboard — same as Week 1. Get all six on the board first, then we look at the pattern together.

Room debrief

Across six businesses, what moved?

The unlock

What was the single input that changed the most — and did it come from the room, the pair, or the deadline?

The stall

Where did people consistently get stuck? If four of six stalled in the same place, that's a format problem, not a discipline problem.

Right constraint?

Was the challenge you named in Week 1 actually your #1 — or did the real one surface in Week 3?

Four weeks

Too short, about right, or too short only for certain kinds of problems? Be specific — this shapes Cohort 02.

The format

Intros, pairs, working blocks, the Friday post — which earned its time and which would you cut?

The room size

Six operators, 90 minutes. Did every problem get real airtime, or did some weeks run thin?
