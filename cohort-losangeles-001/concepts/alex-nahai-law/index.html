06 — Fees

How we bill, stated plainly.

Fixed monthly for ongoing counsel

Retained clients pay one predictable figure. The scope is written down and reviewed with you quarterly as the business changes.

Flat or capped on transactions

Deal work is quoted before diligence begins. If scope changes materially, you hear it before the work happens — not on the invoice.

Hourly only where warranted

Disputes carry a budget and a monthly forecast. You should never be surprised by a legal bill.

Why → Fee opacity is a leading reason companies leave a firm. Publishing the billing philosophy pre-qualifies the buyer, shortens the sales conversation, and signals the kind of relationship on offer. It costs nothing, and competitors won't copy it.
