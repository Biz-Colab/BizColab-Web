What this is

The room is the product.

This isn’t a course, and I’m not the product. It’s six operators pushing each other’s businesses forward, on purpose, every Monday, with a real deliverable at the end.

Not

A course

Networking

House rules

Six things that make this work.

Show up

All four Mondays. Missing one costs the room more than it costs you.

The room stays in the room

Numbers, deals, struggles — nothing leaves without permission.

Experience over advice

“Here’s what happened when I tried it” beats “you should.”

Make specific asks

“Help me grow” gets nothing. “Who’s hired a fractional CFO?” gets three names.

Give first

Offer the intro, the template, the contact before you’re asked for it.

Notes only

A note-taker writes up decisions, asks and commitments. That’s all that leaves the call.

Your three minutes

Three questions, that’s it.

01 · ~60 sec

What you do

  • The business, who it’s for, roughly how big
  • Skip the origin story — save it for Slack
02 · ~90 sec

Your one challenge

  • The thing actually keeping you stuck — pick one
  • Say it as X to Y by when, with today’s number
03 · ~30 sec

What would help most

  • The one kind of help you’d take right now
  • The more specific it is, the more this room can do with it

Too vague: “grow revenue” or “figure out my pricing.” Specific enough: “from 2 inbound leads a month to 8, by Oct 26.” Hard timer, three minutes — hold your advice for the four blocks that follow. Not sure yet? Say your best version out loud and sharpen it in Slack by Wednesday.
