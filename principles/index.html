What we've learned so far

The thing you've been
meaning to do.

You already know what the problem is. You've known for years. What's missing isn't more information. It's a deadline, a room that calls it straight, and one testable move a week. Here's what works.

Part one · Get honest

Start with the real problem.

Most owners spend months fixing the easy thing so they don't have to look at the hard one. The first week is about saying it out loud.

01
🎯

Name the real problem

Not the one that's easy to say. The one you've been working around for years. If it feels a little embarrassing to say, you're close.

TryWrite one sentence: "The thing I keep avoiding is ___."
02
🧭

Work on it, not in it

Running the business and building the business are two different jobs. Block the time for the second one or it never happens.

TryProtect 90 minutes a week. Same day, same time.
03
🪑

Build your board

Five peers outside your industry beat one expert inside it. They don't share your blind spots, and they don't need you to look good.

TryList five people who'd tell you the truth. Not friends. Peers.
04
🗣️

Say it to the right person

Clarity shows up when you state the problem to someone who'll push back. Not your spouse, not your team. Someone with nothing to lose.

TryExplain your problem in 60 seconds. Watch where they get confused.
Part two · Make it move

Four weeks, not four years.

Big plans die in the planning. Short deadlines and small tests are how things actually change.

05
⏱️

Set a short, fixed deadline

Four weeks is long enough to do something real and short enough that you can't put it off. Open-ended goals stay open.

TryPick a date 28 days out. Tell someone.
06
🧪

One test per week

Don't plan the whole thing. Pick one assumption, test it this week, bring back what happened. Then pick the next one.

Try"This week I'll find out if ___ by doing ___."
07
📣

Nobody knows you exist

Most of the time the product is fine. The problem is reach. Whatever you think "enough" outreach is, it's about a tenth of what it takes.

TryCount last week's real outreach. Now 10x it.
08
🏷️

Fix the price before the pitch

More marketing on the wrong price or the wrong customer just makes the problem louder. Positioning and pricing come first.

TryWho's your best client? Charge the next one like them.
09
🔁

Referrals over ads

The clients you already have are the growth engine you keep ignoring. Ask them. Build the ask into how you work.

TryCall three happy clients this week. Ask for one name each.
10
🚫

Keep it separate

One focus, one lane. Mixing businesses, audiences, or offers feels efficient and reads as confusing. Pick the one that matters this month.

TryCut one thing from your plate for 4 weeks. See what happens.
Part three · Make it stick

Candor is the product.

The room only works if people are honest. Honesty only works if trust is designed in.

11
🤝

Design for trust

Be clear about what's shared, with whom, and when it's deleted. People open up when they know the rules. Vague rules get vague answers.

TryBefore asking for honesty, say what you'll do with it.
12
✍️

Your words, not theirs

Own the goal and the progress in your own language. Nobody should summarize your business for you. If it doesn't sound like you, it isn't yours yet.

TryRewrite your goal until you'd say it to a client.
13
🤖

Keep the human parts human

Automate what doesn't need judgment. Keep the conversations, the feedback, and the follow-up personal. People can tell.

TrySend one note this week with no template.
14
📅

Show up every week

The commitment to the room is the real work. Missing one week breaks the chain for you and for the people counting on your feedback.

TryCommit to all four. Not "most of them."
15
🌱

Keep the board

Four weeks starts it. The compounding happens after, when the people who watched you commit are still there to ask how it went.

TryBook the next check-in before the last session ends.
How it works at BizColab

The principles, on a calendar.

Six owner-operators, one facilitator, four weekly 90-minute sessions. Each week picks up where the last one left off.

Wk 1

Name it

Say the real problem out loud to five people who'll push back. Leave with one sentence.

Wk 2

Focus it

Pick the one move that matters. Set the test for the week. Cut what's in the way.

Wk 3

Say it to the right person

Take it outside the room. Clients, prospects, partners. Bring back what happened.

Wk 4

Make it stick

Lock in what worked. Decide what happens next. Keep the board.
