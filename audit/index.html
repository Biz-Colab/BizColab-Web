The audit stands on its own. If you want help following through, you can add a 30-day check-in and a 90-day re-score, or ongoing accountability check-ins and advisory with Michael.

We use it only to write your audit. Working files, notes and the call transcript are deleted within 7 days of delivery. You keep the audit.
