What we look at

Three lenses on the same business. Most founders can do one of these themselves. Almost nobody does all three.

01
How you show up

Can a buyer find you?

Where you land in search for what you do in Los Angeles, your Google profile and reviews, your LinkedIn and social, and whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude name you when asked who’s best in your category.

02
Brand and communication

Does it all say the same thing?

Logo, typography, color and tone of voice across your site, your profiles and your emails. Whether a stranger can tell what you do and who it’s for in 15 seconds, and where the message drifts.

03
Marketing and sales

Is the engine built to grow?

Your offer, your price, who it’s for, where leads come from and what happens after they land. The numbers from your call, and the bottleneck they point to.

Questions

Do I have to start with the free one?

No. If you already know something is stuck, go straight to the Deep Audit. The snapshot is for founders who want a read before they commit.

Is the free snapshot really free?

Yes. You fill out a form, we research your business and send you a one-page snapshot. There’s no call and no pitch. If the Deep Audit would help, we’ll say so at the bottom of the page and leave it there.

What makes the Deep Audit deeper?

The calls. Thirty minutes with Michael to start, transcribed, so the audit is built on your real numbers and your own words instead of a form. Then a 30-minute review call once you’ve read it. Plus the full check on how you show up online, your brand kit, a 90-day plan and copy you can use the same day.

What does the brand and communication check cover?

Your logo, typography, color and tone of voice, and whether they match across your website, LinkedIn, social profiles and emails. We also check that the message says who you’re for. If something major is working against you, it’s the first thing you read in the audit.

Can you redesign my website or brand?

Yes, as part of advisory. The audit tells you what needs to change. If you want BizColab to build it, we scope that separately once you’re in.

How does advisory work?

Two 45-minute working sessions a month, every other week, to review what moved and set the next moves. Between sessions, you can email Michael. Every quarter you get a fresh plan and a new score on the same five areas. $499 a month, month to month.

Who does the work?

Michael, the business advisor behind BizColab. We use research and transcription tools to work fast, and Michael reviews and edits everything before it goes out.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to write your snapshot or audit. Working files, notes and call transcripts are deleted within 7 days of delivery. You keep what we send you.
