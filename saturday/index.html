FAQ

The honest answers.

How is this different from the four-week cohort?

Same framework, same group size, one day instead of four weeks. The Saturday Cohort is for founders who want one focused push. The four-week cohort is for founders who want weekly accountability while they ship.

Where is it?

On Google Meet, Saturday from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm Pacific Time. You join from anywhere with a laptop, a camera and a steady connection.

Is there an in-person option?

Yes, as an optional extra. After the session, founders who live near each other can meet up in person, based on where the group is. We help pick a neighborhood spot. It is not required.

Is there prework?

Yes, about 30 minutes. It's a short set of questions about your business, your numbers and what feels stuck. Your facilitator reads it before Saturday.

What should I bring?

A laptop with a camera, your current numbers (rough is fine) and one problem you want solved.

What if I can't make it?

Your seat moves to the next Saturday Cohort, or your payment counts toward a four-week cohort.

Will there be networking?

Yes, the whole session. You'll work closely with five other founders, and many BizColab members keep working together after their cohort ends.
