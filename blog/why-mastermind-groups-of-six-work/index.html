Why a mastermind group of six works better than a big one

By BizColab · November 10, 2026
The short answer

A BizColab cohort has six business owners and one facilitator. Six is enough voices to challenge you and few enough that nobody can hide. Every owner reports every week, so goals turn into finished work.

Most business groups get bigger as they grow. Ours stays small on purpose.

Every BizColab cohort has six business owners and one facilitator. That is seven people on the call, and it is the most important design decision we made.

Enough voices to challenge you

One advisor gives you one opinion. A room of six gives you six, and they come from different businesses. Someone in the room has already solved the thing you are stuck on, or has a sharper question than the one you asked yourself.

Few enough that nobody hides

In a group of thirty you can show up, nod, and leave. In a group of seven, everyone is on the hook. When it is your turn to report, the room notices. That pressure is the point. It is what turns a good intention into finished work.

One facilitator keeps it moving

The facilitator is not there to lecture. They keep time, ask the next question, and make sure every owner leaves each call with a clear next step.

Who is in the room

Founders and operators running real, revenue-generating businesses. Different industries, same problem: it is hard to grow alone. You can see what past members said on our reviews page.

How a cohort runs

Four weeks. One weekly group call, plus daily check-ins between calls. The weekly arc is Map, Match, Move, Measure. We explain each step in the one-goal rule.
