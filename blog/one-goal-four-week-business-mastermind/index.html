The one-goal rule: how a four-week business mastermind works

By BizColab · November 10, 2026
The short answer

BizColab runs four weeks: Map, Match, Move, Measure. In week one each owner picks one high-leverage goal. The next three weeks are partner work, execution, and a review that ends with a 60-day plan.

If everything is a priority, nothing gets finished. So the first thing we do in a cohort is narrow the list to one goal.

Week 1: Map

Each owner diagnoses their business and picks one high-leverage goal. High-leverage means the thing that makes other problems easier or makes them disappear. It is usually not the loudest problem. The room helps you find the real one.

Week 2: Match

You get paired with a partner and build something for each other. Working on someone else's business is a fast way to see your own blind spots. It also gives you an accountability partner who knows your goal.

Week 3: Move

Execution week. You do the work, report back, and pressure-test the plan with people who will tell you the truth. Daily check-ins keep the pace up between calls.

Week 4: Measure

You review what worked and what did not, then plan the next 60 days. You leave with results and a plan, not just notes.

Why a month works

A month is short enough to commit to and long enough to finish something. It is also short enough that the goal cannot drift.

Four focus areas

Four themes run through every cohort:

  • Implementing AI in the business
  • Compounding processes and tasks
  • Accountability partners and the group
  • Building a business that is sustainable and sellable
