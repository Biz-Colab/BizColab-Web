Why this page?

One look across web and video

Someone who sees the site and then a video should feel the same brand. So the video kit reuses the site: white and light gray, Geist type, one blue, one orange, and the serif italic for a single human word.

The face is the star

Talking faces beat graphics. Every overlay stays out of the middle of the frame and out of the platform caption zones. Graphics support the speaker, they never replace them.

Built to be reused

Every piece scales as one unit and uses the same few parts, so a new episode means changing the words, not redesigning. Once you pick a direction, we lock it as the template for the editing config.

Captions and labels
Six founders and one facilitator.

Geist 500. Plain sentences, no jargon.

1

No black or dark backgrounds. White, light gray, or a saturated blue or orange field.

2

One orange word or object per frame. Orange is the “look here” color.

3

Title, subtitle and detail each sit on their own line. Never a single word alone on a line.

4

Keep overlays out of the speaker’s face and out of the caption zones.

5

The logo is always the wordmark with the blue dot. It never sits on a busy photo.

6

Faces only, never a table, never stock. Props are real objects on a real surface.

PartSpecWhy
Ease incubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 350–450 msFast start, soft landing. Feels confident, not flashy.
StaggerLines 90–120 ms apartThe eye reads top to bottom without help.
HoldTitles 2.5 s, lower thirds 4 s, chapter cards 1.5 sLong enough to read once, short enough to keep pace.
Ease out250 ms, no bounce, move 20% then fadeExits are quiet so the face takes over again.
TransitionOrange wipe left to right, 700 ms, or a hard cut on the beatSame direction as the site menu. No spins, zooms or glitches.
LimitTwo moving things on screen at once, maxMotion supports the speaker, never competes.
